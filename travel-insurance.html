Why the gap exists
⚠️ Confirmed from the actual Certificate of Coverage
GeoBlue's own legal text: "Eligible Dependents must be traveling with the Eligible Participant." Vitaliy is the Eligible Participant — so he's always covered. Rimma, Aden, and Eliot all qualify as dependents, but only when physically traveling with Vitaliy on the same trip. This isn't a marketing simplification — it's the binding plan language, checked against the real Jump Operations LLC Certificate (4EL-6021-26).

Jump's domestic PPO/HDHP plan was also checked directly — it has zero international provisions (strictly In-Network / Out-of-Network within the US). GeoBlue is the only international mechanism Jump provides, so there's no fallback through the domestic plan either.
⚠️ The CSP evacuation fallback is conditional, not automatic
Vitaliy's CSP gives $100k evacuation to Rimma and Luda even without Vitaliy present — but only if the trip is charged to his CSP specifically. If a solo trip is booked on Rimma's Venture X instead, that coverage is $0 — Venture X has no evacuation benefit at all. This is easy to get wrong by default and shouldn't be relied on as the primary safety net.
The fix
✅ Standalone annual multi-trip medical policies
Same model already proven with Luda's IMG GlobeHopper Senior Multi-Trip (~$264/yr, $250k medical, $250k evacuation, $0 deductible). For Rimma, Aden, and Eliot — none qualify for the Senior tier (65+ with Medicare), so use a standard adult multi-trip plan instead, e.g. IMG Patriot Multi-Trip or equivalent. Removes all dependency on remembering which card a trip was charged to.

What to look for in a policy
Checklist for any standalone policy
Medical treatment: $100k–250k minimum — this is the piece nothing else covers
Evacuation: $100k+ built in, so it doesn't depend on which card a trip was charged to
Annual multi-trip vs. single-trip: annual is cheaper once someone travels solo 2+ times/year
Deductible: $0 preferred — Luda's plan set this precedent
Eligibility: confirm no age/Medicare-style gating (only relevant for GlobeHopper's Senior tier)
Repatriation of remains: usually bundled with evacuation — check it's included
